# Fastshot | AI Mobile App Builder

[Fastshot](https://fastshot.ai/) helps you build native **iOS and Android apps by chatting with AI**. Describe your idea, refine the design, and turn it into a mobile app built with **React Native and Expo**. No coding experience is required to get started.

## From idea to app stores

- **Build through conversation.** Describe your app's purpose, screens, and features, then ask for changes as you go.
- **Preview and test.** Review your app in the browser and test it on a physical device with Expo Go.
- **Connect your backend.** Add authentication, a database, and storage with Supabase.
- **Add monetization.** Connect subscriptions and in-app purchases with Adapty, or ads with Google AdMob.
- **Prepare for launch.** Build and publish your app to the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Fastshot supports projects such as productivity tools, community apps, fitness trackers, and marketplaces. Developers can work with the generated React Native code to extend their apps.
